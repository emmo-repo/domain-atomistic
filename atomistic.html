PESStationaryPoint#

IRI https://w3id.org/emmo/domain/atomistic#EMMO_00aebe47_f165_4a69_b70c_443282097c83
Annotations
prefLabel PESStationaryPoint
elucidation A point on a potential energy surface at which the first derivatives of the potential energy with respect to all nuclear coordinates are zero.
Formal description
subClassOf StationaryPoint
Subclasses PESSaddlePoint, PESMinimum
Restrictions
  • isPartOf some PotentialEnergySurface

Note

A stationary point corresponds to a molecular configuration for which the energy gradient vanishes. Stationary points include minima and saddle points.
